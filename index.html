Welcome to ONEIL

Everything runs right here on your computer, no internet needed.
Add some songs to the library, load one on each deck, and you're mixing.

How it works

LOADING SONGS

  • Add songs to the library, then press A or B, or drag a song onto a deck.
  • You can also drag music files from your computer straight onto a deck.
  • The BPM is worked out automatically. If it's wrong, use TAP (tap along 4+ times), ÷2 or ×2. Press GRID on a downbeat to line up the beat grid, or open the Beat Grid panel to fine-tune it.

SYNC, CUES & LOOPS

  • SYNC matches this deck's tempo and beat to the other deck. Moving either pitch fader keeps them together.
  • CUE: when stopped, sets the cue point. Hold it to preview. While playing, it jumps back to the cue and stops.
  • Hot cues: tap an empty pad to save a spot, tap it again to jump there. With the deck stopped, hold a cue to play from it and let go to snap back. Shift-click or right-click a cue to remove it. Cues are saved for each song.
  • Auto loop: pick a length in beats. Tap the same length again, or EXIT, to leave the loop.

STEMS (NEURAL MIX STYLE)

  • Stems button: pick 2 to 4 stem files of one song (for example vocals, drums, bass, other). Files with “vocal”, “drum” or “bass” in the name are sorted automatically.
  • To make stem files, use a free stem splitter such as Ultimate Vocal Remover.
  • Quick Split splits a normal song into Vocals, Drums, Bass and Melody. A rough split starts straight away, then a better one is worked out in the background (the badge shows Splitting… %) and swaps in by itself. It works best on songs with clear drums and a centred vocal.
  • Each stem has M mute, S solo and FX. Only stems with FX on go through the deck's echo and reverb, so you can echo just the vocals.
  • Echo Out Vocals sends the vocals into the echo and drops them on the next beat, so the echo tails away.

SERATO-STYLE PERFORMANCE

  • Sampler settings (in the drop-down under the pads): for each sample choose Tap: play to end, Hold to play (stops when you let go) or Tap on / tap off, turn Loop on, and drag the length slider to choose how long it plays.
  • Pad modes: Hot Cue, Roll (hold for a stutter that snaps back), Auto Loop, Sampler (8 sample pads shared by both decks) and Stems. In Stems, the top 4 pads switch Vocals, Drums, Bass and Melody on and off, and the bottom 4 echo that part out on the next beat.
  • Slip: when it's on, loops, held hot cues and censor let the song carry on underneath, so you land back in time when you let go.
  • Censor: hold to play backwards, then it drops back in on time.
  • Sync ▾ (top bar): choose Beat sync (speed + beats locked) or Tempo only; follow the other deck or a Master BPM you set; and Smart half / double time so an 85 BPM song syncs with a 170 BPM one.
  • Quantize (top bar): hot cues, loops and jumps snap to the beat.
  • Loop: 1 to 32 beat auto loops, or set your own with IN and OUT. RE (reloop) brings back your last loop.
  • DOUBLE: copies the other deck's song, in the same spot and playing, for instant doubles. You can also drag the song title from one deck onto the other, or load the same song.
  • Key: every song's musical key is detected, shown on the deck and in the library as both name and Camelot number (like Am · 8A).
  • Library: click a column name to sort. Drag a column name left or right to move that column (right-click a column name to put them back). Prepare holds songs you plan to play next. History lists what you've played.
  • Audio (top bar): choose speakers, a headphones output for cueing, or send Deck A and B to separate outputs for your Rane One's built-in mixer.

ANALYZE FILES

  • Click Analyze files in the library to work out the BPM, key and length of every song in the list you're looking at (All songs, a crate, Prepare or History).
  • Or drop songs straight onto Analyze files — from your computer or from the library — to add and analyze just those. Drops while it's busy join the queue.
  • It skips songs that are already done. If everything's done, click it twice to redo them all.
  • Results are saved, so you only analyze each song once. You can keep mixing while it works.

CROSSFADER STYLES

  • Pick a style in the drop-down under the crossfader. Basic styles change the fade curve. Neural Mix styles move the stems (vocals, drums, bass, melody) in and out at different points as you slide. Effects styles add a filter, echo, reverb or EQ swap as you fade.
  • Neural Mix styles turn on Quick Split by themselves. They work in whichever direction you're mixing, A to B or B to A.
  • The styles work on the on-screen crossfader and on the Rane One crossfader when it's mapped in Controller. They don't apply in the Separate decks audio mode, where the Rane One mixes the sound itself.

BEAT GRID

  • If the beat lines don't sit on the kicks, pick Beat Grid in the drop-down under the pads.
  • SET HERE makes the spot under the playhead beat 1 (red line). Yellow lines mark each bar. +1 BEAT moves beat 1 along if it landed on the wrong beat.
  • Drag the big waveform left or right to slide the grid onto the beats, or use ◀ ▶ (hold Shift for tiny steps). Outside the panel, Shift + drag the waveform does the same.
  • Grid drifts later in the song? Set beat 1 near the start, play a minute or more in, stop right on a beat and press MATCH BEAT HERE. The BPM is stretched to fit.
  • Type an exact BPM or use − + (0.01 steps, Shift for 0.1). Turn on CLICK to hear a tick on every beat. RESET goes back to the app's own grid. Your grid is saved with the song.

VIDEO

  • Add music videos (MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM) to the library like songs. They show a red VIDEO tag and load onto a deck the same way.
  • Videos work best as MP4 with H.264 video and AAC sound. AVI, MPG and WMV videos show an orange CONVERT tag because browsers can't play them. Videos made with H.265 (HEVC), often from phones, play their sound but the picture may not show. Convert those to H.264 MP4.
  • Press ▶ Video at the top to see the video mix. It follows your crossfader, or pick Cut, Deck A only or Deck B only. Songs without video show their title.
  • Pop-out opens the video in its own window. Drag it onto a TV or projector and double-click for full screen.

LIBRARY SIZE

  • Drag the little bar just above the library up to make the library bigger. The decks and mixer shrink to make room. Drag it down to give them their space back.
  • Click ⤢ Expand in the library (or double-click the bar) to make the library as big as it goes. Click it again to go back. The Practice and full layouts each remember their own library size.

PRACTICE LAYOUT

  • Click Practice at the top for slim decks and a big library, so you can see lots of songs while you practise. The decks keep play, cue, sync, the song info and a small waveform, and the mixer keeps its channels, crossfader and Neural Mix styles. The crossfader always shows in Practice, even with the Mixer button off. Click it again for the full layout.
  • When you plug in your Rane One, the app switches to the full layout by itself. Unplug it and it goes back to Practice. You can always switch by hand with the button.

SERATO DJ CRATES

  • Close Serato DJ, then click Serato above your crates and Choose _Serato_ folder. It's in your Music folder (C:\Users\you\Music\_Serato_), or at the top of a USB drive if your music is there. You only do this once.
  • Get crates from Serato brings in all your Serato crates and subcrates. Add your music folder with + Add music folder so ONEIL can find the songs. Songs it can't find yet fill in by themselves when you add the folder.
  • Send my crates to Serato writes your ONEIL crates as Serato crates. Open Serato DJ and they're there. A Serato crate with the same name gets replaced. Smart crates aren't included.

CRATES

  • Click + New, type a name and press Enter. Double-click a crate to rename it. Click the little square next to a crate (or right-click the crate) to colour-code it or add a subcrate.
  • Drag crates up and down to reorder them. Drop a crate onto the middle of another crate to put it inside as a subcrate. Click ▸ / ▾ to show or hide subcrates. A crate shows its own songs plus everything in its subcrates.
  • Drag songs from All songs onto a crate, or use the + Crate menu next to a song.
  • Use + Add music folder in Chrome or Edge and the app remembers your folder, so your crates fill up again next time.

RANE ONE / CONTROLLER

  • Close Serato first, then plug in the Rane One and click Controller at the top.
  • Click Start guided setup and press each control it asks for. It's saved after that.
  • To hear the music through the Rane One, set it as the sound output in your computer's sound settings.
  • Grab the waveform or the on-screen platter and drag to move the song back and forth, like holding a record. Let go and it plays on. With Platter touch set up, touching your Rane One platter does the same.
  • Controllers need Chrome or Edge.

KEYBOARD

  • Deck A: Q play · W cue · A sync · 1–4 hot cues
  • Deck B: P play · O cue · L sync · 7–0 hot cues
  • ← → move the crossfader · ↑ ↓ pick a song · ? this help
  • Shift + ← load the picked song on the left deck (A) · Shift + → load it on the right deck (B)

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Changing the pitch also changes the key (there's no key lock yet).
  • For headphone cueing, pick a headphones output in Audio, then press CUE on a mixer channel (Chrome or Edge).
  • REC records your mix and downloads it when you stop.
  • Works best in Chrome or Edge. Safari works too.

Controller setup

Connecting…

Guided setup asks you to press or move each control on your Rane One, one at a time. Press Skip this one for anything you don't want to use. You can also click Learn next to any single control below. Your setup is saved, so you only do this once.

SYNC OPTIONS
Sync type
Beat sync matches the speed, lines up the beats and keeps them locked together. Tempo only matches the speed and leaves the beats where they are.
Follow
With Master BPM, every deck with SYNC on plays at this tempo. Change it here and both decks follow.

Audio setup

With your Rane One, pick it as the main output and choose Separate decks. Then the Rane One's faders, EQs, crossfader and headphone cue work like they do with Serato.
For cueing with the on-screen mixer: pick a second output for your headphones, then press CUE on a mixer channel.
